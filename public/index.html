Scoring corners, edges and surface…
How the estimate works: it takes the LOWEST of centering, corners, edges and surface, because one bad corner or an off-center card pulls the whole grade down. Centering limits are PSA's (front 55/45 for a 10, 60/40 for a 9, 65/35 for an 8, 70/30 for a 7, 80/20 for a 6, 85/15 for a 5–4, 90/10 for a 3; back 75/25 for a 10, 90/10 for 9 and below). This is a guess from a phone photo, not a real grade.
